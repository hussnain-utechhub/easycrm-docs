# Sharing a report

**What it is.** Giving another company access to a report or dashboard you own.

**What it does.** It grants that company read, or read and edit, on the item.

**Why it helps.** You do not have to rebuild the same report for each company, and you do not
have to ask an administrator every time.

## Sharing one

1. Open the report or dashboard.
2. Click **Share**.
3. Choose the company.
4. Choose **Read** or **Edit**.
5. Click **Save**.

## Who you can share with

Only companies you are allowed to see. A Super Admin sets that ceiling under
[Reports and Dashboards](../administration/reports-dashboards.md) in the Admin Console.

So there are two levels: an administrator decides which companies a folder may reach, and
within that you decide who actually gets it.

## Taking it back

Click **Revoke** beside the company. They lose access immediately.

## Folder access still applies

Sharing one report does not share its folder. Somebody given a single report sees that report
and nothing else alongside it.
