# Filtering a list

A filter hides the records you do not want to see.

1. Click the filter button on the right of the list.
2. Click **Add Filter**.
3. Choose a field, choose how to compare it, and type a value.
4. Click **Save**.

![The Filters panel](../img/shots/lists/filters-panel.png)

The list now shows only the records that match.

## Using more than one filter

Add as many as you need. By default a record must match **all** of them.

To change that, click **Add Filter Logic** and write a rule such as `1 OR 2`. The numbers are
the filters in the order you added them.

## Removing a filter

Open the panel, remove the filter, and click **Save**.
