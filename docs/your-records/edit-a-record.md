# Changing a record

1. Open the record.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Change what you need.
4. Click **Save**.

![Editing a record](../img/shots/records/edit.png)

Click **Cancel** to throw away your changes.

## If a field will not change

Some fields are read-only. Either the field is calculated by the system, or you do not have
permission to change it. Ask your administrator.

## If Save shows an error

The message says which field is wrong. Fix that field and click **Save** again.
