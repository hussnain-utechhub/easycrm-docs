# Getting around

Every page has the same bar across the top.

![The home page](../img/shots/home/overview.png)

| What you see | What it does |
|---|---|
| The tabs | **Home**, your records, **Reports**, **Dashboards**. Click one to go there. |
| **Search** | Finds any record by name. |
| The gear | Changes how the portal looks to you. |
| The bell | Shows your notifications. |
| Your name | Your settings, and **Log Out**. |

## The home page

The home page shows you four things:

- **The number tiles** — how many records of each kind you can see.
- **My Tasks** — tasks that are yours. Overdue ones are marked in red.
- **Recent Records** — what you opened last.
- **Quick links** — shortcuts to each tab.

## Searching

Click **Search** at the top and start typing. Results appear as you type.

![Searching](../img/shots/shell/search.png)

Click a result to open it.

## Notifications

Click the bell to see what has happened recently.

![Notifications](../img/shots/shell/notifications.png)

## The whole home page

![The whole home page](../img/shots/home/full.png)

## Changing how it looks

Click the gear.

![View settings](../img/shots/shell/view-settings.png)

You can put the menu **along the top** or **down the left side**, and pick a colour.

This only changes what *you* see. Nobody else is affected.

## Your account menu

Click your name in the top right.

![The account menu](../img/shots/shell/user-menu.png)

From here you reach **Settings**, **Log Out**, and the spacing of rows on screen —
**Comfy** or **Compact**.
