# Signing in

Your administrator sends you a username and a password by email.

1. Open the web address your administrator gave you.
2. Type your **username**.
3. Type your **password**.
4. Click **Log In**.

![The sign-in page](../img/shots/login/sign-in.png)

You are now on the home page.

## If your password does not work

You see a message in red. Check for typing mistakes and try again.

![A rejected sign-in](../img/shots/login/wrong-password.png)

## If you forget your password

1. Click **Forgot password?**
2. Type your username or your email address.
3. Click **Send reset email**.

![Forgot password](../img/shots/login/forgot-password.png)

A new password arrives by email. Sign in with it, then change it in
[Settings](../your-account/password.md).

Still stuck? Your administrator can set a new password for you.
