# What you can see

Not everyone sees the same things. It depends on your role.

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Standard** | See and change their own records. |
| **Admin** | Everything a Standard user can, plus manage the people at their company. |
| **Super Admin** | Everything, for every company. |

Your administrator chooses your role. You cannot change it yourself.

## Why a tab might be missing

If someone else has a tab you do not have, one of these is true:

- Your role does not include it.
- Your administrator has hidden it for your company.
- You do not have permission for that kind of record.

Ask your administrator. They can turn it on from
[the Admin Console](../administration/console.md).
