9vs Experience Cloud

The first question any Salesforce person asks. Answer it before a competitor does.

Experience CloudEasyCRM
LicensingPer member or per login, on top of SalesforceNo per-user Salesforce licence for portal users
Built forFull communities — content, self-service, forumsRecords, reports and files
SetupSite builder, templates, sharing sets, often a partnerInstall, configure on screen, invite users
Configured byUsually a developer or consultantAn administrator
Multi-companyPossible, typically several sitesBuilt in, one installation
When Experience Cloud is the better choice
If you need a public community, knowledge base, forums or heavy self-service branding, use Experience Cloud. EasyCRM is for giving a lot of people straightforward access to records and reports, cheaply and quickly.
Why concede that

A comparison that concedes nothing reads as marketing. One that concedes something specific reads as expertise — and makes the rest of the page believable.

Verify before publishing

Salesforce licence types and prices change. Do not publish figures without checking them that day, and date the page. A stale competitor price invites a correction that undermines everything else.

11The .ai domain

A fair criticism. Three honest answers, one dishonest one.

OptionMeansCost
A · Build somethingShip one genuine AI feature and lead with itEngineering time
B · Say nothingTreat .ai as a short modern domain; never imply moreFree
C · Change domainMove to .com or .ioDomain + migration
The dishonest option

Sprinkling "AI-powered" over search or filtering. Buyers ask what the AI does within one question, and a vague answer costs more trust than having no AI at all.

If you take option A, these fit the product

CandidateWhy it fits
Ask a question, get a report"Accounts with no activity in 60 days" builds it. The builder exists — this is a front end.
Summarise a recordAn activity timeline into three sentences. Self-contained.
Explain this accessPlain English for "why can they see this?", from data you already compute.
Recommendation

Option B now, option A next. Say nothing about AI until something ships. Then make "ask a question, get a report" the headline — it justifies the domain and is the smallest of the three to build.
